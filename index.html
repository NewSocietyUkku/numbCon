⚠️ Why Julian Day Number?

The Gregorian calendar skipped days historically (1582, 1752, 1918). These gaps make it unusable for continuous itti conversion.

We use the Julian Day Number (JDN) — a continuous count where every day is numbered sequentially with zero gaps — and set the itti epoch at JDN 1,721,426 = 1 January 0001 (the start of the Common Era).
